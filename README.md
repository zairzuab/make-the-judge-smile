## The entry

A single line of at most 200 characters about a small good thing that happened today, something that would make a reader smile. A judge model compares entries two at a time on the published brief: subject first, then warmth, then surprise, then one concrete detail, then fresh wording.

> The cat next door, who has ignored me for two years, trotted over this morning and headbutted my shin like we'd been friends all along.

135 characters. The small good thing: a cat that ignored me for two years finally came over. The warmth: it chose me. The surprise: two years of ignoring, then a headbutt. The concrete detail: headbutted my shin. The fresh wording: "like we'd been friends all along."

## Usage

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (and to `entry.txt` beside the source for review). Node 22, standard library only, no network.
